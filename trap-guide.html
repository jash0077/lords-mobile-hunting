THE ART OF TRAPPING

Master the secrets of Solo and Rally traps to dominate high-might attackers.

Trap Account Strategies

What is a Trap Account?

A trap account is a castle that appears weak (low might) to bait larger players into attacking. Once they attack, they are met with a massive hidden army that caps their hero and destroys their troops.

1. Solo Trap (100M - 250M Might)

Troop Composition

  • T2 Infantry: 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T2 Ranged: 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T2 Cavalry: 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T4 Infantry: 400K - 600K
  • T4 Ranged: 400K - 600K
  • T4 Cavalry: 400K - 600K

Recommended Gear

  • Main Hand: Frostwing Greatsword
  • Helmet: Bumblehelm (Queen Bee)
  • Armor: Firewall Plate (Trojan)
  • Legs: Storm Tasset (Tidal Titan)
  • Off-hand: Winter Mittens (Snow Beast)
  • Accessories: Burning Scroll x3
2. Rally Trap (400M - 600M Might)

Troop Composition

  • T2 Infantry: 6M - 8M
  • T2 Ranged: 6M - 8M
  • T2 Cavalry: 6M - 8M
  • T4 Infantry: 1.5M - 2.5M
  • T4 Ranged: 1.5M - 2.5M
  • T4 Cavalry: 1.5M - 2.5M

Essential Stats

  • Mixed Attack: 450% - 600%+
  • Army HP: 400%+
  • Army Defense: 350%+
  • Anti-Scout: 24/7 Active
3. The "Don'ts" of Trapping
  • ❌ Don't Research Economy: It adds too much might without combat value.
  • ❌ Don't Research Walls: Wall might is "fake might" and useless for trapping.
  • ❌ Don't Upgrade Monster Hunting: Keep it at Level 7-8 to save might.
  • ❌ Don't Forget Anti-Scout: If they see your troops, the trap is ruined.
