// SECTION 01: TRAP FUNDAMENTALS

What is a Trap Account?

A trap account is a strategic deception unit. It projects an image of vulnerability to lure high-value targets (large players) into an engagement. Upon initiation of attack, the trap reveals its true combat capabilities, neutralizing the threat and securing enemy leaders. This is not about brute force; it's about calculated risk and superior counter-intelligence.

CRITICAL DIRECTIVE: AVOID TERRITORY MISSIONS

Do NOT claim territory missions. These generate inflated might without contributing to combat effectiveness, making your account appear less appealing as a target. Maintain a lean, deceptive might profile.

// SECTION 02: SOLO TRAP CONFIGURATION (100M - 250M MIGHT)

Solo Trap: Intercept & Neutralize

Optimized for intercepting single-player attacks. The objective is to cap enemy leaders and inflict maximum troop casualties with minimal personal loss. This configuration thrives on consistent, low-might engagements.

Troop Deployment Protocol:

  • T2 Infantry: 2.5M - 3.5M (Primary Buffer)
  • T2 Ranged: 2.5M - 3.5M (Primary Buffer)
  • T2 Cavalry: 2.5M - 3.5M (Primary Buffer)
  • T4 Infantry: 400K - 600K (Damage Core)
  • T4 Ranged: 400K - 600K (Damage Core)
  • T4 Cavalry: 400K - 600K (Damage Core)

Gear & Stat Prioritization:

Focus on maximizing Army HP and Mixed Attack. Recommended F2P gear includes: Frostwing Greatsword (Main Hand), Bumblehelm (Helmet), Firewall Plate (Armor), Storm Tasset (Legs), Winter Mittens (Off-hand), and Burning Scroll x3 (Accessories).

// SECTION 03: RALLY TRAP CONFIGURATION (400M - 600M MIGHT)

Rally Trap: Containment & Annihilation

Engineered to absorb and counter multiple coordinated rallies. This requires a significantly deeper troop pool and precise tactical execution.

The "Archer Wedge" Protocol:

For sustained defense against 20+ incoming rallies, deploy an Archer Wedge formation. This leverages a massive ranged buffer to absorb initial impact while your T4 core delivers decisive damage.

  • T2 Archers: 50.0M (Primary Damage Sponge)
  • T2 Infantry & Cavalry: 30.0M Each (Secondary Buffer)
  • T4 Mixed Troops: 6.0M Each Type (High-Impact Damage Core)
  • Minimum T4s: 3.0M Each Type (if supported by superior stats)
// SECTION 04: EFFICIENCY & MIGRATION PROTOCOLS

The Law of Diminishing Returns: Optimize, Don't Overload

Unnecessary might is a liability. Beyond a critical threshold, additional troops or research provide diminishing returns, making migration prohibitively expensive and reducing tactical flexibility.

Example: Increasing training speed from 300% to 600% yields significantly less time reduction than the initial 300%. The same principle applies to troop counts; excessive numbers beyond optimal layering primarily serve as a buffer, not a damage multiplier.

Migration Strategy: Stay Agile

High might (2.1B+) severely restricts migration options. A smart trap prioritizes Efficiency over Raw Power to ensure guild mobility. Avoid being stranded in a stagnant kingdom due to excessive might.

// SECTION 05: BAITING & DECEPTION TACTICS

Operation: Ghost Online (Bait Masterclass)

To successfully bait a rally, you must convincingly appear offline or vulnerable. Every action should reinforce the illusion of an easy target.

  • 🎭 Fake Gear Protocol: Remain in Monster Hunting or Training gear. Execute your gear swap to full War Gear precisely 1-2 seconds before impact. Premature swaps will result in rally cancellations.
  • 🛡️ The Farmer Leader Deception: Deploy a non-combat hero (e.g., Trickster, Scarlet Bolt) on your wall. This signals a lack of defensive awareness, increasing your appeal as a target.
  • 🤫 Silent Running: Under no circumstances should you scout back or engage in chat with the attacking party. Maintain absolute silence to simulate an unattended account.
  • ⏰ Timed Shield Drop: Allow your shield to expire naturally during perceived "sleep hours" within your guild's timezone. A recently unshielded castle is a prime target.
  • 📍 Dead Teleport Maneuver: Relocate to a remote, inactive sector of the kingdom. Remain stationary for at least one hour before dropping your shield to enhance the illusion of an abandoned account.

WARNING: ACTIVE DEFENSE REQUIRED

These tactics are for Active Defense. If you are not online and prepared to counter, you will be burned. Deploy a shield if you cannot actively manage your trap. Ego will not save your troops.

// SECTION 06: PROHIBITED PROTOCOLS

The "Don'ts" of Trap Management

Avoid these common pitfalls that compromise trap effectiveness:

  • ❌ Economy Research: Adds might without combat value. Avoid.
  • ❌ Wall Research: Generates "fake might" and is tactically useless. Avoid.
  • ❌ Max Monster Hunting Research: Keep at Level 7-8 to conserve might.
  • ❌ Neglecting Anti-Scout: Visibility compromises your deception. Maintain anti-scout status at all times.
