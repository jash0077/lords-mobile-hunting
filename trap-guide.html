The definitive guide to castle defense, baiting, and efficient trapping.

The Philosophy of Trapping

A trap account is a masterclass in Deception. Your goal is to appear as a weak, tempting target while hiding a lethal army. Success is measured not by might, but by captured leaders and burned armies.

Crucial: Territory Missions

DO NOT claim territory missions. This is the #1 mistake new traps make. Territory missions add massive "fake might" that makes you look too strong to be hit, without adding any actual combat stats.

The Law of Diminishing Returns

More is not always better. In Lords Mobile, each new improvement helps less than the previous one.

If training takes 6 days, 300% Speed drops it to 3 days (3-day gain).
But 600% Speed only drops it to 2 days (only 1-day additional gain).

The Lesson: Don't over-train troops. After a certain point, you are just creating a buffer to absorb rallies, not increasing your damage output.

Baiting: How to Pretend Offline

1

The "Fake Gear" Swap

Stay in Monster Hunting gear. Only swap to War Gear 1-2 seconds before impact.

2

The Farmer Leader

Use a non-combat hero like Trickster on your wall to look like an easy target.

3

Silent Treatment

Never scout back or message. Act as if you are completely away from your device.

Active Defense Rule

This methodology is for Active Defense. If you are offline, you will eventually burn. If you aren't online to counter, use a shield.
