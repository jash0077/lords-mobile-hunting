THE ART OF TRAPPING

Master the secrets of Solo and Rally traps to dominate high-might attackers.

Trap Account Strategies

What is a Trap Account?

A trap account is a castle that appears weak (low might) to bait larger players into attacking. Once they attack, they are met with a massive hidden army that caps their hero and destroys their troops.

1. Solo Trap (100M - 250M Might)

Troop Composition

  • T2 Infantry: 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T2 Ranged: 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T2 Cavalry: 2.5M - 3.5M

  • T4 Ranged: 400K - 600K
  • T4 Cavalry: 400K - 600K

3. Low Rally Trap Methodology

Crucial First Step

NOTE: Do not claim any more territory missions. This is vital for maintaining a low might profile while having advanced capabilities.

The Law of Diminishing Returns

Stop thinking that sheer numbers will capture an emperor. Troop counts work like research or construction gear—the more you have, the less each additional unit helps.

Example: 300% training speed cuts 6 days to 3 days (3-day gain). But 600% speed only cuts it to 2 days (only 1-day additional gain). The same applies to troops—after a certain point, more troops just absorb rallies rather than increasing damage.

Bait vs. Over-Equipped

There are two ways to play:

  • The Over-Equipped: So many troops that nobody will ever rally you. Safe, but arguably boring. Primarily prevents "Rally Parties" (coordinated simultaneous attacks).
  • The Bait: A castle that looks tempting. This is where the fun is. You want to look like someone who *can* be burned to bait the attack and capture the leader.
4. Advanced Rally Defense & Migration

The Migration Problem

Being "too strong" (2.1B+ Might) makes migrating ridiculously expensive. If your guild moves and you're stuck due to scroll costs, you'll be bored for a month. Smart traps focus on Efficiency over Power to stay mobile.

High-Level Composition (The "Wedge" Setup)

For surviving multiple connected rallies (up to 20+), use an Archer Wedge formation with this setup:

  • T4 Core: 6M of each type (Inf/Ran/Cav). Minimum 3M if you have high stats.
  • T2 Buffer: 30M T2 Infantry & Cavalry, and 50M T2 Archers.
  • Why? The T2 Archers reinforce the Wedge's strength, soaking damage while your T4s deal the killing blows.

Active Defense vs. Offline

This method is for Active Defense. If you are online and countering, you can cap anything. If you are offline, even the best trap can be burned by 10+ consecutive rallies from Titans. If you're not online, use a shield. There is no ego in saving millions of troops.

5. How to Pretend Offline (The Bait Masterclass)

Master the Art of Deception

A trap is only as good as its bait. If the enemy knows you're online, they won't hit. Here is how to look like an easy, offline target:

  • 🎭 The "Fake Gear" Swap: Stay in Monster Hunting or Training gear. Only swap to your real War Gear 1-2 seconds before the rally hits. If you swap too early, they will cancel.
  • 🛡️ The Bait Leader: Put a non-war hero on your wall (like Trickster or Scarlet Bolt). This makes you look like a "farmer" who doesn't know how to defend.
  • 🤫 Silent Treatment: Never scout back. Never message the rally lead. Act as if your phone is in another room.
  • ⏰ Natural Shield Drop: Let your shield expire naturally during "sleep hours" (based on your guild's timezone). A castle that just dropped its shield is the most tempting target.
  • 📍 The "Dead" Teleport: Teleport to a random spot in the kingdom, far from the action, and stay there without moving for an hour before dropping shield.
6. The "Don'ts" of Trapping
  • ❌ Don't Research Economy: It adds too much might without combat value.
  • ❌ Don't Research Walls: Wall might is "fake might" and useless for trapping.
  • ❌ Don't Upgrade Monster Hunting: Keep it at Level 7-8 to save might.
  • ❌ Don't Forget Anti-Scout: If they see your troops, the trap is ruined.
