The War Manual: Trap Guide

Strategic imperatives for dominance, deception, and the art of the ambush.

Operational Briefing

Fundamentals

The Art of the Ambush

A trap account is not merely a castle; it is a meticulously crafted illusion. Its purpose: to project an image of vulnerability, drawing powerful adversaries into a fatal engagement. When the enemy commits, the trap unleashes its hidden might, capturing their leaders and shattering their forces.

Critical Warning

Under no circumstances claim territory missions. These generate deceptive might that inflates your power score without enhancing combat capability. Stay lean to remain a target.

Solo Trap Deployment

100M - 250M Might

Engineered to decisively counter single-player attacks. Your objective is swift leader capture and maximum troop destruction with minimal casualties.

Troop Composition

  • T2 Infantry 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T2 Ranged 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T2 Cavalry 2.5M - 3.5M
  • T4 Infantry 400K - 600K
  • T4 Ranged 400K - 600K
  • T4 Cavalry 400K - 600K

Gear & Stat Prioritization

Focus on Army HP and Mixed Attack. Essential F2P gear: Frostwing Greatsword, Bumblehelm, Firewall Plate, Storm Tasset, Winter Mittens, and Burning Scroll x3.

Rally Trap Deployment

400M - 600M Might

Designed to absorb and crush multiple coordinated rallies. Demands a deeper troop reserve and flawless tactical execution.

The Archer Wedge

Deploy the Archer Wedge to survive 20+ rallies. This leverages an overwhelming ranged buffer to absorb the initial onslaught.

  • T2 Infantry & Cavalry 30.0M Each
  • T4 Mixed Troops 6.0M Each
  • Minimum T4s 3.0M Each

Baiting & Deception

Covert Operations

Project an undeniable image of vulnerability. Every detail must reinforce the illusion of an easy target.

Fake Gear

Maintain Hunting/Training gear. Swap to War Gear 1-2 seconds before impact.

Farmer Leader

Deploy a non-combat hero (Trickster/Scarlet Bolt) on your wall to signal negligence.

Radio Silence

Do not scout or communicate. Simulate an unattended, vulnerable account.

Timed Shield Drop

Allow shield to expire naturally during "sleep hours" to attract opportunists.

Enhancement Protocols

Jewels & Familiars

Jewel Priority

Mixed Attack is paramount. Deploy Infantry, Ranged, and Cavalry Attack jewels across all war gear.

Defensive Familiars

Prioritize HP Buffs and Type-Specific Shields. Use Gryphon, Aquiris, and Terraspike for F2P; Jade Wyrm and Hell Drider for P2P.

Prohibited Protocols

Fatal Flaws
  • Economy Research Avoid
  • Wall Research Avoid
  • Max Hunting Research Limit to Lv 7-8
  • Anti-Scout Always Active
