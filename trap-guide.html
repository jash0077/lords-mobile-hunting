The Concept

Three Core Principles

Every decision must be measured against these three rules of engagement.

01

Stay Small on Paper

Minimize visible might. Avoid unnecessary buildings and research that inflate your power without adding combat value.

02

Stack HP and Defense

Maximize troop health and defense stats. A trap survives the hit and grinds the attacker's army down through attrition.

03

Build for Aftermath

Maximize infirmary capacity. The goal is to convert deaths into wounded, and wounded back into troops rapidly.

Buildings

A trap account's layout evolves. Early speed is replaced by defensive capacity.

Foundation

Expansion

Manors for Training

Run manors during active troop training to maximize speed. These are temporary fixtures.

Mobilization

Infirmary Conversion

Once troop counts are met, demolish manors for infirmaries (Lv 21-25). Minimize deaths at all costs.

Support

Embassy Maximization

High embassy level allows more guild reinforcements—critical for surviving full rallies.

Discipline

Minimum Viable Infrastructure

Skip unnecessary upgrades. Every point of might must be justified by defensive utility.

Talents & Research

The Foundation

Skip the economy tree. Focus on survival, healing, and enemy debuffs.

Survival

Army Health II / Defense II: Core stat boosts. Bigger Infirmary: Essential for capacity.

Tactical

Quick Swap: The heart of the bait strategy. Field Triage: Faster healing efficiency.

Debuffs

Sabotage / Plague / Demoralize: Chip 10% off the attacker's army stats before they land.

Philosophy

The Commander's Mindset

Success is 90% psychology and 10% stats.

Proactive Defense

Be honest about your strength. Mimicking titans without the stats leads to ruin. Align strategy with reality.

The Shield of Wisdom

Active defense only. If offline or facing titans, shield. Progress is more important than ego.

Quality Over Bloat

Invest in research and gear synergy. A lean, high-stat account outperforms a bloated, weak one.

Pre-Fight

Checklist for Baiting

Ensure these assets are ready before you lower your guard.

  • Resource Tokens: Keep resources in bags so they aren't looted.
  • Speedups: Several days saved for post-hit healing.
  • Shields: In reserve for strategic cooling off.
  • Anti-Scout: Prevent enemies from seeing your real troop count.
  • Defense Boosts: Active or ready to trigger immediately.
  • Vault Protection: Keep loose resources below protected limits.
  • Guild Briefing: Ensure reinforcements match your formation.
